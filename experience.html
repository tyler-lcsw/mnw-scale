Professional summary

Trauma care connected to daily life.

Erin specializes in trauma and complex situations. She listens first, asks simple questions to make sure she understands, and allows clients to choose and change their therapy goals. Her EMDRIA certification supports one evidence-based treatment option within a broader, individualized approach.
