Trauma treatment shaped around you

EMDR in the spotlight, with more than one way forward.

Erin offers EMDR therapy online when it is an appropriate fit. EMDR is one of several evidence-based approaches she can draw from, so care does not depend on one method or require you to arrive knowing exactly what you need.

01

Listen first

Erin begins by listening to what is happening now and what feels most important to you.

02

Make sure she understands

She asks simple questions to confirm what you mean without making assumptions about your experience.

03

Let goals stay yours

You can choose your therapy goals and change them as your needs or priorities change.

04

Choose methods with care

Erin may use EMDR and other evidence-based techniques according to your concerns, preferences, and treatment goals.
