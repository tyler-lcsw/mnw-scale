Preparing for a session

A few practical details help online care feel more settled.

Exact platform and scheduling details can be discussed directly with Erin. In general, telehealth works best when a client has a reliable device, internet access, and a private space where they can speak openly and be addressed in the language that fits them.

01

Choose a private location

Use a space where you feel comfortable speaking openly and where interruptions are less likely. Headphones may add privacy when other people are nearby.

02

Use a stable device and connection

A computer, tablet, or phone with a camera, microphone, and dependable internet connection can support a clearer conversation.

03

Keep your location available

Because services are limited to Indiana, be prepared to confirm where you are located during the session.

04

Plan a transition after the session

When possible, leave a few minutes before returning immediately to work, caregiving, driving, or other demands.

What stays personal

Online care can still begin with feeling heard.

Erin listens first, asks simple questions to make sure she understands, and lets you choose and change your therapy goals. The format changes where the conversation happens, while your concerns and priorities remain central.

Trauma-focused care

Sessions can address trauma and complex situations through EMDR and other evidence-based approaches selected for your needs.
