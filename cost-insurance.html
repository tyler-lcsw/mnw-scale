Questions you can ask

Gather the details that affect your decision.

Insurance policies and practice arrangements may change. Confirm information directly with Met Needs and Wellness and your insurance company before relying on coverage or estimating your cost.

01

Which insurance options are currently available?

Ask Met Needs and Wellness for the latest participation and billing information. No plan-specific participation is claimed on this page.

02

What will my plan cover?

Ask your insurer about outpatient mental health services delivered by telehealth while you are physically located in Indiana.

03

What could I owe?

Your insurer can explain your deductible, copay, coinsurance, network rules, and any limits or authorization requirements that may apply.

04

What should I confirm with the practice?

Ask about current fees, payment timing, cancellation terms, and any other practical policy that matters to you.
