Online therapy for adults with intellectual disabilities · Indiana

Care that respects you as an adult and keeps your voice central.

Erin welcomes adults with intellectual disabilities who are looking for online therapy in Indiana. Care can address trauma, complex situations, feeling overwhelmed, relationships, grief, life changes, and other concerns that matter to you.

Support for your concerns

Therapy starts with the person, not an assumption.

Adults with intellectual disabilities have different lives, communication preferences, strengths, concerns, and support needs. Erin takes time to understand what is affecting you and what you want help with. Online therapy and any individual needs can be discussed during the free consultation.

You may also see the broader term intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD) in health care and support settings. Here, the focus is specifically on therapy for adults with intellectual disabilities.

01

Trauma and complex experiences

Difficult events may continue to affect safety, trust, emotions, relationships, or daily routines. Erin provides trauma-focused care and may consider online EMDR when it is suitable for the individual client.

02

Feeling overwhelmed or stuck

You do not need a clinical word for the problem. Therapy can begin with what feels hard now and work toward less overwhelm and a clearer way forward.

03

Relationships and life changes

Care can address relationships, boundaries, grief, changing routines, work or day-program experiences, living situations, health stress, and other changes in adult life.

04

Your choices and goals

Erin asks what you want from therapy, checks that she understands, and makes room for you to change the focus when your needs or priorities change.

Working together

Listening first. Clear questions. Your goals.

Erin’s approach is collaborative. She listens first, asks simple questions to make sure she understands, and works with you to decide what matters in therapy. You can discuss individual communication and support needs during the consultation.

You lead your therapy

Your preferences and goals stay at the center of the work. Having support in other parts of life does not erase your voice in therapy.

Ask about trusted supporters

If a family member, friend, support worker, or professional is part of your support system, you can ask Erin how their role may be handled while keeping your choices central.

Fit is considered individually

The free consultation is a place to discuss online access, schedules, questions, and whether Erin’s care may fit your needs.
