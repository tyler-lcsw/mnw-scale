Later life can hold many kinds of change

You deserve room for the full situation.

A concern does not become less important because it is connected to aging or a change others expect. Therapy can give you a private place to consider what has happened, what feels difficult now, and what you want to be different.

01

Trauma and complex experiences

Past or recent experiences may affect safety, trust, relationships, or daily life. Erin offers trauma-focused care and may use online EMDR when it fits your needs and goals.

02

Grief and accumulated loss

Loss may involve people, health, abilities, routines, community, roles, independence, or the future you expected. You do not have to make the experience fit a prescribed timeline.

03

Health stress and care transitions

Changes involving health, housing, transportation, caregiving, or support needs may bring uncertainty, frustration, relief, grief, or several responses at once.

04

Relationships, identity, and changing roles

Retirement, partnership, family roles, caregiving relationships, identity, and community connections may shift. Therapy can help you consider what you need and how you want to respond.

Senior counseling, centered on the client

Your voice remains central.

Erin’s background includes outpatient therapy, crisis care, community mental health, and extensive work with older adults. Care remains guided by your priorities. If trusted people or other professionals are part of your support system, you can ask how their role may be handled.

You choose your goals

Therapy begins with what matters to you, and you can change the goals as your situation or priorities change.

Ask about your support system

You can ask Erin how a trusted family member, friend, support worker, or professional may fit into the process while keeping your choices central.

Online care removes the trip

Eligible adults can meet with Erin from a private place in Indiana when telehealth is suitable and the technology works for them.
